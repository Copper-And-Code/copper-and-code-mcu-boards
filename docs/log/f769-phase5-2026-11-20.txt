Date: 2026-11-20. Owner present and agreed; the owner used Board Explorer and touched the panel.
Board: STM32F769NI, ST-LINK/V2.1 (/dev/ttyACM0 = UART1 REPL). Final firmware sha256
a787d51d263c5136..., written with st-flash --connect-under-reset, verified.

== machine (MicroPython 1.29's own)
Pin, ADC, PWM, I2C, SPI, UART, Timer, WDT, RTC are there. machine.ADC has no read_uv (read_u16).
ADC on A0-A5: A0-A2 on ADC1, A3-A5 (PF10, PF8, PF9) on ADC3, chosen by machine.ADC from the pin.
PWM on the connector (machine.PWM's own pick): D0 TIM8_CH2, D1 TIM8_CH1, D3 TIM10_CH1, D5 TIM8_CH3,
D6 TIM11_CH1, D9 TIM12_CH1, D10 TIM1_CH4, D11 TIM12_CH2, D12 TIM12_CH1, D14 TIM11_CH1,
D15 TIM10_CH1, A0 TIM13_CH1, A4 TIM13_CH1, A5 TIM14_CH1. Described: D3 D5 D6 D9 D10 (no bus pins).
- UART6 added on D1 (PC6 TX) / D0 (PC7 RX) in mpconfigboard.h: UART(6) did not exist.
- I2C1 on D15/D14 (PB8/PB9), SPI2 on D13/D12/D11 (PA12/PB14/PB15), D10 PA11.
- machine.Pin("PA6") did not exist (the port knows "A6" and the board's names; "A0" is the
  connector's PA6, not PA0). pins.csv now also names the connector's pins, the LEDs and the user
  button by their MCU names ("PA6,PA6", ...).
- PWM: going from 50 Hz to 1 kHz with init(freq=) or freq(), the pin stayed low for a while: a
  lower prescaler takes effect at the counter's next overflow, up to 0.65 s at the old one. A new
  PWM object starts at once.

== Description: python/mpy_board.py + boards/stm32/STM32F769_DISCO/mpy_board_data.py (frozen)
The format and the API of lvgl-mpy-linux's mpy_board (describe, pin, connector); /flash/board.json
replaces the frozen one. Connector "Arduino" D0-D15, A0-A5; i2c 1, spi 2, uart 6; pwm 5 pins with
the timer's name; adc 12 bits 3.3 V with channels; aliases; LEDs LD1 red PJ13, LD2 green PJ5 (LD3
PA12 is D13, left out); button PA0.
Checked on the board: every connector pin opens by name, the 6 ADC inputs read, the 5 PWM pins
start (timer names as described), UART(6), I2C(1).scan() (nothing wired: []), SPI(2), LEDs, button.

== Touch calibration (the LED switches at the bottom were hard to hit)
Nine crosses (3 x 3), the owner touching each; one touch was counted twice (the centre), so the
bottom-right cross is missing. With the earlier four targets, 21 touches:
  screen y from raw x: current 479 - raw has residuals up to +53 at the bottom (reads too high)
  fit: y = 516.0 - 1.0925 * raw x (rms 16.1 px);  x = -19.2 + 1.008 * raw y (rms 11.7 px)
In ft6x06.FT6x06 as cal=(1.008, -19.2, 1.0925, -7.3) after swap_xy and invert_y (stm32_board.py).
The rest is the fingertip. Owner after it: LED switches at the first touch, pins right.
